The Founder

Every love affair has a beginning

Nude Amore is the newest chapter in a house built on obsession. London-based founder Duncan Nyanzi has spent over a decade in the world of fine wine and sparkling — always chasing the same thing: a bottle so clean and alive that nothing else compares.

Nude Amore is that pursuit poured into Prosecco — a Valdobbiadene Superiore DOCG born to give the finest hills in Italy the seduction they have always deserved.

"So clean, so alive, so effortlessly sexy that nothing else compares. It's Prosecco — but not as you know it."

Duncan Nyanzi · Founder of Nude Amore

The Story So Far

It began with rosé

Before Nude Amore came Chelsea Water — our Provençal rosé, taken from zero to national distribution and a Channel 4 broadcast placement. It's a Cru Classé Côtes de Provence, made for us by Château de Saint-Martin in Taradeau: a 100-hectare estate of near-mythic pedigree, where the Romans first planted vines and Lérins monks built an underground cellar still in use today. The same noble family has passed it down since 1740, countess to countess, now into its eleventh generation. Quite simply, some of the very finest producers in Provence.

And then, Champagne

Charles Duncan

Our own grower Champagne — a Brut and a Brut Blanc de Blancs, produce of France — crafted with Champagne Colette & Gaston, a family house in Monthelon in the heart of the Côte des Blancs, where tradition and modernity meet in every cuvée.

From Provence rosé to Champagne to the Prosecco of Valdobbiadene, the thread is the same: work only with the very best growers, and bottle something worth remembering.
