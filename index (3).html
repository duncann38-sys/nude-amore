An Untold Story

Beneath the Dolomites

In the hills of Valdobbiadene, beneath the Dolomite mountains of north-east Italy, whole villages live and breathe the making of Prosecco — a craft handed down through generations and still barely known to the world beyond. Nude Amore was born to tell that story: to take the finest expression of these hills and give it the seduction it has always deserved.

II — The Estate

Born on the most seductive hills in Italy

After a long search for a home worthy of the name, we found it high on the hill of Col Vetoraz — the very peak of the Conegliano-Valdobbiadene appellation, beside the fabled slopes of Cartizze. Here one family has tended the vines since 1838, and the winery has drawn more than 250 awards, becoming a benchmark for this magnificent land — now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The vineyards sit at almost 400 metres, on soils of limestone and sandstone, where warm days and cool nights let the Glera grape keep its freshness and build its perfume. The slopes are far too steep for any machine — every bunch is picked and sorted entirely by hand, plot by plot, so only the finest fruit ever makes the blend.

Obsession For Detail

Nothing added. Nothing taken away.

Each harvest is vinified parcel by parcel, so the character of every plot can be read before a single blend is chosen. Native yeasts begin the first fermentation; the second is drawn out slowly in temperature-controlled tanks over thirty to forty days, then rested on its lees to build that creamy, seductive mousse. No fining, no invasive treatment, no shortcuts — only what the Glera gives, protected and poured.
